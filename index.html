II. Reading

12 pts

Port Selma

Where is it? Port Selma is a small fishing town on the coast, about two hours from the capital.

What can you see? The best-known place is the old tower, the tallest building in town. From the top, you can see the whole town and the sea. Next to the port there is a busy fish market that opens every morning at six o'clock.

When to visit: Summers are sunny and hot, but winters are cold and rainy, so most visitors come between December and March.

Culture: Every February the town has Fishermen's Day. People paint their boats and put flags and lights on them. There is also a big party in the streets with live music.

Getting around: The town is small, so most people walk. There is also a free bus that goes to the beaches.

Alta Vista

Where is it? Alta Vista is a town in the mountains in the north. It is bigger than Port Selma, with about 30,000 people.

What can you see? The most popular place is the Sunday market in the main square, where local people sell clothes and toys that they make. Near the square there is a big park with a small lake, and families often relax there.

When to visit: The weather is the same all year, so you can visit at any time. The nights are cold, so bring a warm jacket.

Culture: Every August the town has Music Week. Groups from all over the country play music in the streets and in the main square.

Getting around: Two minibus lines go from the old town to the hills. The ride takes ten minutes.
